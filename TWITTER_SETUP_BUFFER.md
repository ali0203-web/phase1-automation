## 📋 STEP 1: Create Buffer Account

1. Go to: https://buffer.com
2. Click "Get Started"
3. Sign up with email (takes 2 minutes)
4. No credit card needed (ever)

---

## 🔗 STEP 2: Connect Twitter to Buffer

1. In Buffer dashboard, click "Add Channel"
2. Select "Twitter/X"
3. Click "Authorize with Twitter"
4. Approve Buffer access to your Twitter account
5. Done! ✅

## 📅 STEP 4: Schedule Tweets in Buffer

### 4a. Add First Tweet
1. In Buffer dashboard, click "Create Post"
2. Select "Twitter"
3. Paste Tweet 1
4. Click "Add to Queue"

### 4b. Set Schedule
1. Select "Schedule"
2. Pick a time (e.g., Tuesday 9am)
3. Click "Schedule"

### 4c. Repeat for All 5 Tweets
- Schedule them on different days
- Spread throughout the week
- Mix up the times

### Example Schedule:
```
Monday 9am     → Tweet 1 (Introduction)
Wednesday 12pm → Tweet 2 (Career)
Friday 9am     → Tweet 3 (Learning Path)
Monday 3pm     → Tweet 4 (Community)
Wednesday 5pm  → Tweet 5 (What Makes Different)
```

## 📊 TRACK ENGAGEMENT

### In Buffer Analytics
1. Dashboard → Analytics
2. See engagement for each tweet
3. Identify what resonates
4. Replicate successful tweets

### Track These Metrics
- **Impressions** - How many saw it
- **Engagement Rate** - Likes + retweets + replies
- **Clicks** - Traffic to links
- **Follower Growth** - New followers per day

## 💡 TIPS FOR VIRAL TWEETS

### What Works on Twitter
- ✅ **Numbers** - "14 weeks", "$600K+", "6,100+ lines of code"
- ✅ **Stories** - Personal wins, student successes
- ✅ **Questions** - Engage audience ("What's your AI goal?")
- ✅ **Visuals** - Add images (career path graphs, code examples)
- ✅ **Value** - Tips, insights, learnings
- ✅ **Authenticity** - Real struggles and wins

### What Doesn't Work
- ❌ Generic marketing
- ❌ No clear value
- ❌ Too many hashtags
- ❌ Spam-like content
- ❌ Low engagement rate

### High-Performing Tweet Template
```
[Hook: Surprising fact or question]

[Context: Why it matters]

[Evidence: Proof or example]

[CTA: What to do next]
```

Example:
```
Most AI engineers make $80-120K.
The top 1% make $600K+.

The difference isn't IQ. It's having built real systems.
Our curriculum teaches that.

14 weeks. 14 projects. Real production experience.

Ready? [LINK]
```
